Motivazione del committente

The tender is awarded to the company Syspons GmbH. The winner convinced thanks to a solid track-record in meta-evaluation, evaluations synthesis and M&E systems, a diverse and complementary team, strong technical proposal, and competitive financial offer.

Descrizione

Service 1: Rapid quality assessment of evaluations (SECO-WE: 48 days / SDC: 112 days); Service 2: Rate DAC criteria of evaluations (SECO-WE: 48 days / SDC: 112 days); Service 3: Synthesize contents of evaluations (thematic/content analysis) (SECO-WE: 48 days / SDC: 152 days); Service 4: Synthesize results of services 1-3 and triangulation (Synthesis Report) (SECO-WE: 60 days / SDC: 112 days).
