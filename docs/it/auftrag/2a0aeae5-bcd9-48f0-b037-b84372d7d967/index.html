LIFT pilot project for drone-based postal delivery in the Maldives

Ente pubblico
Union postale universelle
10.11.2026, 17:00Scadenza
Documenti e pubblicazione ufficiale su simap.chAggiungi la scadenza al calendarioSegnala un errore

Descrizione

This call for tenders concerns the provision of services to the UPU to execute a pilot project to implement unmanned aircraft systems (UAS) to deliver logistics services for the designated operator of the Maldives. The total duration of the pilot project, including preparations and actual test flights, shall be 10 months.
