Begründung des Auftraggebers

Los 1

The tender is awarded to the company Syspons GmbH. The winner convinced thanks to a solid track-record in meta-evaluation, evaluations synthesis and M&E systems, a diverse and complementary team, strong technical proposal, and competitive financial offer.

Los 2

The tender is awarded to the Consortium GOPA Worldwide Consultants GmbH and CEval GmbH. The winner convinced thanks to a solid experience in impact and ex-post evaluations employing multiple approaches and methods, rapid evidence reviews, trainings and more broadly evidence-based programming, an exceptional team and strong technical proposal.

Beschreibung

Los 1

Service 1: Rapid quality assessment of evaluations (SECO-WE: 48 days / SDC: 112 days);
Service 2: Rate DAC criteria of evaluations (SECO-WE: 48 days / SDC: 112 days);
Service 3: Synthesize contents of evaluations (thematic/content analysis) (SECO-WE: 48 days / SDC: 152 days);
Service 4: Synthesize results of services 1-3 and triangulation (Synthesis Report) (SECO-WE: 60 days / SDC: 112 days).

Los 2

Service 5: Methodological support of high-quality impact or ex-post evaluations (SECO-WE: 140 days);
Service 6: Support evidence-informed programming (SECO-WE: 140 days);
Service 7: Support preparation of independent thematic evaluations (SECO-WE: 28 days).
