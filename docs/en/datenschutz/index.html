What personal data does this site contain?

This site reproduces official publications from simap.ch. Most contract winners are companies. Some, however, are sole proprietorships or self-employed individuals: in those cases the name of a natural person appears together with the contract, the amount and the public buyer. We do not collect contact persons, email addresses or telephone numbers separately. Where the text of a tender contains them, we reproduce them unchanged from simap.ch.

Purpose and justification

The purpose is to make award decisions that have already been officially published findable and comparable. Federal, cantonal and communal authorities published this data under public-procurement law, and it is publicly accessible. We reproduce it without changes to its content, under the simap.ch API terms of use. The processing rests on the overriding interest in the transparency of public procurement.

Data about visitors

This site sets no cookies, uses no analytics or tracking services, and embeds no third-party scripts or fonts. There are no user accounts. The only form is the optional subscription to Tenders by email (see below). The site is served through GitHub Pages; the hosting provider may record technical connection data such as IP addresses in its own server logs.

Tenders by email and RSS feeds

To receive new tenders by email, subscribe with the form on the Tenders by email page. Your subscription only becomes active once you click the confirmation link we email you (double opt-in). We process your email address, the cantons and sectors you choose, your language, the time and IP address of your confirmation and, for no more than 45 days, the list of tenders already sent to you, so that none is sent twice. We use all of this solely to send these emails, on the basis of your own subscription. The emails contain a tracking pixel and links that pass through a Brevo server, so Brevo may record opens and clicks. You can prevent this by turning off automatic image loading and opening the tenders directly on auftragsregister.ch. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in every email or by sending an email with the subject “Stop”. We then remove you from the mailing list within 30 days and delete the list of tenders sent. Brevo keeps your contact record (including your email address and the time of subscription and confirmation), a record that you unsubscribed and the logs of the emails already sent, to make sure you receive no further emails. We do not sell the data, pass it on to third parties for their own use, or use it for any other purpose. It is processed on our behalf by Brevo (Sendinblue SAS, Paris, EU) for subscriptions and sending and, for emails sent to abo@auftragsregister.ch (error reports included), by ImprovMX Inc. (USA), which forwards them, and Google LLC, which hosts the mailbox (Gmail). The RSS feeds require no registration and send us no personal data.

Archive and retention

We refresh the data from simap.ch, usually Monday to Saturday. Older publications stay in the archive, even once they can no longer be queried through the simap.ch interface.

Your rights

You may at any time request access to the data concerning you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and object to the processing. A short email to the address above is enough; we handle requests free of charge. The publication on simap.ch remains authoritative: you should also ask the authority that published it for a correction.

Source: simap.ch

This site presents officially published data and does not replace simap.ch. Only the publications on simap.ch are authoritative.
