How to read this list

1,435 tenders from the Confederation, cantons and communes whose deadline is still open, published on simap.ch. We sort them by deadline and group them by canton and sector.

The tenders that close first are at the top. Each row opens the tender: public buyer, deadline, category (CPV code) and a link to the official publication. Canton pages show the tenders in your region, sector pages those in your line of work: construction, engineering, IT, insurance, vehicles and more. The data comes from simap.ch, the official platform. We usually update it Monday to Saturday, once simap.ch has released the day’s publications.
