Get new tenders automatically

New tenders appear on simap.ch every morning. Choose your cantons and industries, and we will send you the new ones – free, no account needed.

Sign up (free)

Cantons (none selected = all of Switzerland)
Industries (none selected = all)

You will first receive an email with a confirmation link; the subscription starts only once you click it. After that, you will receive an email on days when new tenders matching your selection appear, and none on other days. You can unsubscribe at any time via the link in every email. Sign-up and sending are handled by Brevo (EU). Privacy

Or sign up by email

Send an email to abo@auftragsregister.ch with the subject “Abo”, stating the canton (e.g. ZH) and the industry (e.g. construction or CPV 45). You will receive a confirmation and then an email on days when new matching tenders appear. To stop, send an email with the subject “Stop”. Your address is used only for this and is never shared.

By RSS feed (instant, no sign-up)

Every canton and industry page has its own feed. Add its address to your feed reader, to Outlook (RSS subscriptions) or to an RSS-to-email service.
