Description

The planned new Allmend primary school building aims to create a future-proof, sustainable, and pedagogically functional infrastructure for primary grades 1 through 6. The four-story building will be constructed using conventional timber framing on the school grounds, replacing the existing outdoor sports facility ("Red Square"). The geological and hydrogeological conditions on the site are considered challenging. In particular, two separate groundwater aquifers must be taken into account. The soil conditions necessitate a pile foundation (concrete displacement piles), including timber piles for utility lines and inspection chambers. With regard to flood protection, the new building meets the requirements for a 300-year flood event (HQ300). Building services will be located on the ground floor and top floor (no basement) to prevent damage from flooding. The building's load-bearing structure is based on a solid timber frame made of spruce/fir. This is complemented by three solid service cores made of reinforced concrete. The spiral staircases between the service cores are also constructed of reinforced concrete. The facade consists of prefabricated timber-frame exterior elements. The building is certified according to SNBS 2.1 building construction standards (aiming for Gold certification). Energy is supplied via a connection to the local district heating network SeeEnergy (heating in winter, cooling water in summer). All surfaces are equipped with underfloor heating. All floor coverings are made of polished industrial concrete, serving as thermal mass and providing gentle free cooling during summer overheating. Temperature is controlled by room thermostats via preset temperature settings. A building-integrated photovoltaic system is installed on the roof to generate electricity and also functions as the roof covering. Metal brise-soleil structures along the east and west facades act as shades and provide structural weather protection for the facade. Mechanical ventilation is planned only for the interior cores and the auditorium. Classrooms receive fresh air via manual natural window ventilation. The building is fully accessible. The planned school building, with a total height of approximately 19 meters and constructed using hybrid methods, is classified as a medium-rise building. Fire protection planning complies with quality level QSS 2 of the VKF (Association of Cantonal Fire Insurance Companies) fire protection guidelines. The landscaping is based on the "Center 1st + 2nd Phase" design concept and contributes to the development of a unified public open space in the center of Horw. The school playground will be designed as a traffic-free, publicly accessible space, taking into account its use by extracurricular activities. The school garden is exclusively for school use and is designed accordingly, with a focus on children and natural features, in line with climate adaptation and biodiversity promotion principles.
