Gemeinde Oetwil am See, Beschaffung ICT-Betrieb und Infrastruktur

Public buyer
Gemeinde Oetwil am See
11 Dec 2026, 23:59Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

The municipality of Oetwil am See intends to transfer its entire ICT to a new service provider as part of a full outsourcing, as the contract with its current partner expires at the end of 2028. The new solution is to go live on 1 January 2029 for all seven sites: the Municipal Hall, the Old Municipal Hall, the Works Depot/Water Supply, the School Administration, the Swimming Pool, the Fire Brigade and the Library. The core of the project is migrating the existing cloud environment to the provider's future environment. There, the provider will operate Microsoft 365 with Teams telephony and SharePoint, the records management solution GEVER365 and around 25 specialist applications. The project also includes a network renewal on a rental basis, with uniform Wi-Fi and guest Wi-Fi at all sites. In addition, the provider will procure 65 workstation setups, each with a laptop, docking station, two monitors and peripherals, and deliver them fully configured. The one-off project services include project management, data migration, a test phase, a rollback plan, documentation and training. The provider will then run day-to-day operations as a managed service, including client management, second-level support and above, backup, and encrypted communication with residents. The procurement does not cover the municipal administration software Innosolv, the licences for the specialist applications, or the printer fleet.
