Energieverbund Goldach: Verteilnetz

Public buyer
Politische Gemeinde Goldach, vertreten durch die Technischen Betriebe Goldach
23 Dec 2026, 23:59Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

The Regional Water Supply St. Gallen (RWSG) is currently constructing the new Riet II lakewater plant. As part of this project, the municipality of Goldach has the opportunity to make use of the new lake water intake and thereby establish a thermal network. Using large-scale heat pumps, lake water can be utilized as a renewable energy source to provide environmentally friendly heating for households, public buildings and commercial premises.

The project is divided into two sub-projects:
Sub-project 1 – Energy centre
Sub-project 2 – Distribution network

The basic contract covers SIA sub-phase 31 (preliminary design) – distribution network.

The option covers SIA sub-phases 32–53 for Zone 1 and the district cooling.

The general planner’s mandate covers the following services and specialist areas:
- Overall management and coordination
- Civil engineering
- Pipeline construction
- private connections
