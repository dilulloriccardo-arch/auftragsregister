Description

ETH Zurich is seeking a solution for issuing and verifying digital credentials based on the Swiss federal government's trust infrastructure. The solution must be adaptable to ETH's needs and suitable for use cases in the field of education. A high degree of international compatibility and flexibility are expected. With this project, ETH aims to replace a large proportion of the certificates currently issued and, in the long term, also qualifications with digital credentials.
