Framework agreement for the replacement and renewal of door locking systems for the Municipality of Dottikon, Canton of Aargau

Public buyer
Gemeindeverwaltung Dottikon
9 Nov 2026, 16:00Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

The Municipality of Dottikon is procuring, through a selective procedure, a multi-year framework agreement for the phased renewal and expansion of door locking and access control systems in its properties. The scope of services includes project design and planning, supply, installation, integration and commissioning, instruction and documentation, as well as maintenance and fault rectification. The services will be procured on an object-specific basis through individual call-offs as required. The existing dormakaba exos infrastructure must be taken into account.
