Description

Lot 1

The requested air freight transport services mainly involve imports to Norway, Finland, Belgium and Israel, but also exports to Israel and South Korea.

The goods to be transported are in the following categories:
· general merchandise (consumer goods, spare parts, machinery, textiles, etc.), including dangerous goods in classes 2–6.2 and 8–9
· Materials subject to authorisation (dual-use or other military equipment) including dangerous goods in class 1.4S
· War material according to the EU Military Goods List (weapons and weapon components)

Lot 2

Air freight transport services are requested from the USA within the framework of Foreign Military Sales (FMS). The requested air freight transport services include consolidating all outgoing deliveries from the USA in a central warehouse and then shipping them collectively by air freight to Switzerland. Shipments are also regularly sent back to the USA for export. The transport service must also include intra-US transport by truck or integrator.
