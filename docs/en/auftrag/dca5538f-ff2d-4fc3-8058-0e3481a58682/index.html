Replacement of UCC Infrastructure

Public buyer
Schweizerische Epilepsie Stiftung
22 Jan 2027, 12:00Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

The subject of this procurement is the replacement of the existing telephony infrastructure of the Swiss Epilepsy Foundation. The procurement covers a future-proof, comprehensive telephony solution, including the required hardware and software, licences, end devices and clients. The scope of services also includes design, delivery, integration, migration, commissioning, training and handover to operations, as well as operation, maintenance, support and lifecycle management for the duration of the contract.

Bidders can find detailed information on the subject of the procurement in the specifications (Pflichtenheft) and the list of annexes.
