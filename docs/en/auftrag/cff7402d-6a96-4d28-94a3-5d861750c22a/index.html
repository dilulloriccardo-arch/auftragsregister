Catering (meals) for the day-care structures at Oberglatt Primary School

Public buyer
Primarschule Oberglatt
11 Jan 2027, 12:00Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

Primarschule Oberglatt is inviting bids through an open tender procedure for lunch catering at four childcare locations during 39 operating weeks per school year. The contract starts on 1 August 2027 and runs for three years, with an option to extend by two years. Demand is expected to grow, but no minimum purchase volume is guaranteed.

Complete bids must be submitted in German in a sealed envelope marked “Verpflegung Tagesstrukturen” and received by 11 January 2027 at 12:00 noon. Bidders must demonstrate relevant catering experience, financial stability, suitable staff and infrastructure, insurance coverage, and compliance with participation conditions. Consortia, subcontractors and alternative bids are not permitted. Award criteria are set out in the tender documents, and a site visit and tasting are planned for February or March 2027. Offers and invoices must be in Swiss francs. Swiss law applies.
