Description

The mobile radio licences that were awarded to Swiss mobile network operators in 2012 will come to the end of their term in 2028. The expiring frequencies are to be put out to public tender in 2026 and allocated by auction in 2027. OFCOM is seeking support in preparing the auction rules and organising an electronic frequency auction.
