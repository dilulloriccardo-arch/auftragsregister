Procurement of a Weed Control System

Public buyer
Rhätische Bahn AG
9 Dec 2026, 23:59Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

As part of the ongoing renewal of vehicles and equipment for infrastructure maintenance operations of the Rhaetian Railway (RhB), a spraying system for vegetation control is to be procured.

The contractual scope of services includes, in particular:
- Engineering and detailed design
- Manufacturing and CE certification
- Delivery to the designated site
- Commissioning
- Training of operating personnel
