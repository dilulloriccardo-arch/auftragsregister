Wohnen am Stadtpark: BKP 281.0 Screed Flooring

Public buyer

14 Dec 2026, 16:00Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

The subject of this tender is the installation of screed flooring for the new construction of a residential and care centre for the elderly, comprising 24 nursing rooms, 60 senior living apartments, a restaurant and associated ancillary spaces.

The building consists of one basement level, a ground floor and five upper floors. The basement, ground floor and first upper floor will be constructed using conventional solid construction. The second to fifth upper floors will be constructed using timber construction.

The detailed scope of services is set out in the tender documents.
