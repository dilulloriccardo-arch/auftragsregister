Ozonation (ozone generator, sparging system, residual ozone destroyers)

Public buyer

11 Nov 2026, 11:00Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

The Rorguet wastewater treatment plant is to be expanded to include a fourth treatment stage based on ozonation, followed by the addition of powdered activated carbon. This tender covers the system for the production of ozone, its injection into the reactor and the destruction of excess ozone.
