Client Representation & Project Management for the New SZU Headquarters and the New Maintenance Facility

Public buyer
Sihltal Zürich Uetliberg Bahn SZU AG
8 Jan 2027, 16:00Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

ENG: The existing maintenance facility at the Giesshübel site in Zurich has become technically and operationally obsolete and no longer meets the future requirements of modern rolling stock.

The project предусматриes the dismantling of the existing facility and the construction of a new, future-proof maintenance facility comprising two railway tracks, each approximately 130 metres long, as well as a washing facility. The project is situated in a densely built-up urban environment with severely limited space and complex boundary conditions. We are seeking an experienced project management / client representation team to provide overall project leadership and ensure optimal support throughout this multifaceted and complex project. The objective of this procurement is to appoint an experienced project management / client representation provider to offer comprehensive support to the client throughout the planning and implementation of the new maintenance facility, ensuring effective management and control of costs, schedules, quality and risks. Further details regarding the exact scope of services can be found in document B1_Lastenheft.
