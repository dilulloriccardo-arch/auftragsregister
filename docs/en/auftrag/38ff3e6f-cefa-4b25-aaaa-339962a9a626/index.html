Description

The STZ plans to procure new intensive care beds over the next few years that meet the hospital’s high quality standards. The intensive care beds currently in use have reached the end of their service life and must be replaced. As part of this request for proposals, a supplier will be evaluated that can deliver, within the specified timeframe, a market-proven intensive care bed that is fluoroscopable at chest level with a C-arm.
