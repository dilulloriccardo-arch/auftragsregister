Description

Aarau Cantonal Hospital (KSA) is continuously advancing the digitalization of its HR processes. Today, various HR processes are still handled administratively or with limited digital support. To increase efficiency and improve the user experience for both employees and managers, Employee Self Services (ESS) and Manager Self Services (MSS) based on SAP Fiori are to be introduced and further expanded.

For the design, implementation, and rollout of these solutions, KSA is seeking an experienced service provider with proven expertise in SAP HCM and SAP Fiori.
