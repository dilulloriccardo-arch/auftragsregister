Evaluation of ICT Infrastructure Sourcing Partners for the Municipality of Uitikon

Public buyer
Gemeinde Uitikon (Metagon AG)
4 Dec 2026, 12:00Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

Through this request for proposals, the municipality of Uitikon is seeking an experienced IT sourcing partner who understands the

IT needs of a municipality. The sourcing partner will be responsible for operating the entire ICT infrastructure: the data center (including server infrastructure), digital workplaces, network (WAN, LAN, WLAN), and the Security Operations Center.

As part of a transformation and transition process, the provider will migrate the existing infrastructure to managed services, utilizing its private cloud in Switzerland. The provider will partially procure new network components (routers, switches, firewalls) and client devices (laptops and desktops).
