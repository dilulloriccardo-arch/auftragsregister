Description

The properties owned by the Municipality of Kaiseraugst are equipped with mechanical and mechatronic locking systems. Various systems have reached the end of their service life, and renovation work is being planned and carried out as part of several sub-projects. A new locking system, covering all properties belonging to both the residential municipality and the citizens' municipality, is to be procured to replace the existing systems and equip new buildings.

The planned replacement is a hybrid system combining a mechanical locking setup with an electronic access control system featuring access readers, electronic door hardware, and electronic digital cylinders.
