Description

All business travel activities worldwide are handled by SGTC for employees of the Federal Administration. This means that SGTC supports the administrative units and offers all services related to travel management, such as flights, trains, hotels, rental cars, transport options, etc. The aim of the upcoming tender is to find a reliable and competent contractor for the issuance and management of Lodge Card. The contractor is required to provide a suitable evaluation and management system that supports the daily work of SGTC so that all travel-related costs and activities as well as invoices can be viewed, consolidated and easily evaluated online.
