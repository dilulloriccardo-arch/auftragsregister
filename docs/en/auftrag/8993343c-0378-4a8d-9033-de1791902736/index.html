Description

The new buildings at the Grabs hospital complex consist of two structures: Building S and Building O. Building S is being constructed in three phases, with Building O being built concurrently with Phase 2 of Building S. Phase 1 of Building S opened in September 2020. Phase 2 and Building O are scheduled for completion by the end of 2027. Subsequently, the third and final phase of Building S will take place, with the facility expected to become operational around 2032. The client reserves the right not to proceed with specific works or phases.

The works described below concern the landscaping and site development for Building O, the oncology garden, and the inner courtyard
