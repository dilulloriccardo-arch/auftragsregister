Procurement and pre-deployment of Chromebooks and Chrome tablets for Zollikofen schools

Public buyer
Einwohnergemeinde Zollikofen
8 Dec 2026, 12:00Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

Supply contract for Zollikofen schools: 850 new Chromebooks (684 for primary school and 166 for lower secondary school), each with a compatible active stylus and an integrated storage compartment; 15 new Chrome tablets for primary school; power adapters and charging accessories; and 865 Chrome Education Upgrades. The contract includes zero-touch pre-deployment for two existing Google organisations (699 primary and 166 lower secondary devices), electronic device lists, physical separation and labelling by school level, and delivery and handover at the central delivery location. The sole option is 8 GB instead of 4 GB RAM for all 850 Chromebooks; it must be priced separately and is not evaluated. Partial offers are not permitted. Minimum requirements and the scope of services are set out in the specifications available on simap.ch.
