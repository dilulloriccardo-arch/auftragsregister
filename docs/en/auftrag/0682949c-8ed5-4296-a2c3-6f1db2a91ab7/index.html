Description

This call for tenders concerns the provision of services to the UPU to execute a pilot project to implement unmanned aircraft systems (UAS) to deliver logistics services for the designated operator of Bhutan. The total duration of the pilot project, including preparations and actual test flights, shall be 16 months.
