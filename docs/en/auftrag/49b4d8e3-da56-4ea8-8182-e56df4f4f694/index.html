Description

The cEvo research group is looking for qualified software development specialists to support projects on an on-call basis. The aim is to develop and operate web front-end and back-end applications, data pipelines and database engines, as well as to provide and maintain servers. We are looking for application developers (senior to expert) and DevOps engineers (senior) with proven experience in accordance with Swiss ICT standards. Calls are made according to actual demand, and providers ensure long-term deployment planning and availability. Framework agreements (up to three) with flexible hourly quotas are to be concluded.
