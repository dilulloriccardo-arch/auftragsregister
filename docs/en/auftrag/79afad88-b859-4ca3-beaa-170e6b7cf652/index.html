Maintenance and repair of gas detection and alarm systems

Public buyer
HBD - Immobilien Stadt Zürich (IMMO)
13 Jan 2027, 10:00Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

This invitation to tender is for one contract per lot for the maintenance and repair of gas detection and alarm systems, including the fault response and on-call services described in Part C (service specification). Lot 1 covers the systems managed by Immobilien Stadt Zürich (IMMO) and Lot 2 those managed by Liegenschaften Stadt Zürich (LSZ). Each contract is expected to run for three years, with an option to extend it for a further two years.
