Steffisburg/Thun; Delivery of VRS-T/BLS pressure pipes, fittings, and valves in DN400

Description

The client renews and expands its water distribution network annually.

The pipe replacement involves a section that will be integrated into an existing pipeline network. To ensure the network’s structural integrity and uniformity, VRS-T/BLS FZM/ZMU pressure pipes, fittings, and valves in DN400 are specified.

This is a fundamental technical decision by the client. For the products required for this purpose,

the client intends to select a supplier with whom it will enter into a one-year framework agreement for the supply of these products. The draft framework agreement is included in this request for proposals.

(Further details are provided in the documents) (See Part E).
