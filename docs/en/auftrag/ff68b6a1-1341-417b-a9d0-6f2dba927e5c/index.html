Description

As part of the new construction of the Rebweg senior living facility in Küsnacht ZH, the electrical installation works are being carried out. The works include the supply and installation of the entire high-voltage and low-voltage systems, including the required cable routing, riser zones, and distribution boards. Also included is the installation of all end consumers (lighting, sockets, switching devices) as well as the connection to the building services systems. Commissioning, functional testing, as well as the safety certification (SiNa) and documentation of the electrical system are part of the scope of services.
