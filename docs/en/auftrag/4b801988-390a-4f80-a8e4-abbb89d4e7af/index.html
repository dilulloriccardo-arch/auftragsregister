Rail maintenance vehicle 20xx crane module

Public buyer
Regionalverkehr Bern-Solothurn AG
29 Jan 2027, 16:00Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

As part of the procurement of the new Xa731-741 service railcars, new modular work equipment is required. This includes the replacement of the two cranes currently permanently mounted on railcars X701 and X703.

The scope of supply comprises the design, fabrication, commissioning, and certification/approval of the crane modules in conjunction with the Xa731-741 service railcars. Two (2) identical crane modules shall be delivered.

The modules shall be built on a base frame with overall dimensions derived from a standard ISO container.
