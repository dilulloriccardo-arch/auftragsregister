Description

This tender is for the procurement of racks, PDUs and cooling units, including the associated services, for the new data centre.
The scope of the tender comprises the supply, delivery, installation and handover of infrastructure components in a ready-to-operate condition (commissioning). This includes both passive and active components, as well as services:
· Passive components: Delivery and assembly of racks and containment systems
· Active components: Delivery and installation of PDUs. Delivery, hydraulic connection and integration of RDHx (active), in-row coolers (IRCs) and recirculating air coolers (ULKs).
· Services: Commissioning, documentation and assistance with the technical integration into the overarching building management system (BMS).
To this end, the PSI will conclude a framework agreement with a maximum of three suppliers. The specific services will be procured via project-specific mini-tenders.
