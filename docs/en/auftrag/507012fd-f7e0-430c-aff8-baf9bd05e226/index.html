Drones

Public buyer
Kantonspolizei Zürich
18 Dec 2026, 14:00Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

The Zurich Cantonal Police use drones for various policing tasks. To ensure the sustainable operation and continuous further development of drone operations, the contracting authority is seeking capable framework agreement partners to provide support. The goal of the procurement is not merely the delivery of products or services, but the establishment of partnerships that will provide the Zurich Cantonal Police with professional and technical guidance in the professionalization and further development of their drone operations.

The procurement is divided into three lots:
Lot 1 - Drones & Accessories
Lot 2 - Maintenance
Lot 3 - Training
