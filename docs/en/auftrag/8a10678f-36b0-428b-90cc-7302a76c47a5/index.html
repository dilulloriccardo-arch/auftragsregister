Fernwärme Wil, civil engineering TP11.01 Center South

Public buyer
Stadt Wil, Technische Betriebe, TBW
21 Dec 2026, 17:00Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

Technischen Betriebe Wil (Wil Municipal Utilities) are planning a district heating network supplied by waste heat from the ZAB waste incineration plant in Bazenheid. A transfer pipeline, approximately 5 km in length, will transport the heat to the southern part of the city of Wil. From there, it will be distributed to customers via two main pipelines: one extending westward to the St. Gallen Psychiatric Services facility, and the other eastward—passing the Lindenhof school complex—to the Wil Hospital.

This tender concerns civile engineering for sub-project 11.01, "Zentrum Süd" (Center South), which encompasses a section of the "West" main pipeline. The total length of the route to be constructed (excluding individual building connections) is approximately 800 meters.

The project also includes the renewal of gas and water pipelines.

As part of this project, the southern edge of the "Stadtmarkt" bus stop will be upgraded to ensure accessibility for persons with disabilities.

The construction work will also affect several traffic signal installations.
