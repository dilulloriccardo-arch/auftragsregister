Steelwork for bus stop Bärenplatz in Kreuzlingen

Public buyer
Bauverwaltung Kreuzlingen (Grosjean Architekten AG)
29 Jan 2027, 12:00Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

The bus stop consists of a main frame made of hollow-section steel with two columns. The columns are fixed transversely. In the longitudinal direction, the main girders are rigidly connected to the columns. At the base, the frame is connected with a hinge in the longitudinal direction. The secondary girders are fixed at both ends to the roof girder. A lightweight steel structure is attached all the way round the roof, to which the membrane and the electrical equipment required for operation are fitted. A photovoltaic system is mounted on the secondary roof girders, which also serves as weather protection.
