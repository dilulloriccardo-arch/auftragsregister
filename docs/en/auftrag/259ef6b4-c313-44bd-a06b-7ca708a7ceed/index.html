CoolCity, TP0 surveying services for the client/developer

Public buyer
Elektrizitätswerk der Stadt Zürich (ewz)
12 Nov 2026, 12:00Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

Within the city centre of Zurich, in the area between Zurich Main Station and Bürkliplatz, and between the Limmat River and the Schanzengraben, as well as in the adjacent Brandschenke district, the CoolCity energy network is to be developed to provide heating and cooling. Lake water will serve as the source of both heating and cooling.

The purpose of this tender is to procure surveying services on behalf of the client for Subprojects 1 and 2.
A detailed project description and the scope of services requested are set out in Document 5.
