Description

Flughafen Zürich AG is looking to procure sanitary supplies such as consumables, spare parts, and hygiene components efficiently and reliably. To this end, it is seeking qualified suppliers who can deliver these items on a long-term basis and meet the requirements of security-critical airport operations. Suppliers are sought who can provide a wide range of sanitary products, guarantee stable supply chains, and ensure professional support. High product quality, availability, transparent prices, and reliable delivery to Zurich Airport AG are important factors. The plan is to conclude a five-year framework agreement with one supplier to ensure the long-term supply of sanitary supplies.
