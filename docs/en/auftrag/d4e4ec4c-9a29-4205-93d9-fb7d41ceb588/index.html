Description

Principal and ancillary services, including protective and auxiliary measures, for the construction of the Bicycle Station 2 enclosure in accordance with the project documentation.

Structural steel assembly
- Expanded metal façade, including supporting structure and sub-framing
- Roofing, including waterproofing and secondary supports
- Edge, protective, and cover flashings, including sub-framing
- Roof drainage; gutter, including plumbing lines within the enclosure
- Cable safety system and roof access ladder
- Steel railings for access ramp; bird-proofing for enclosure
- Production planning, Workshop planning, etc.
