Replacement of the GIS system at Gemeindewerke Pfäffikon ZH. (Systems and services such as GeoDataServices and staff cover arrangements)

Public buyer
Gemeindewerke Pfäffikon
18 Nov 2026, 14:00Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

Gemeindewerke Pfäffikon (gwp) intend to replace the existing geographic information system (GIS) and the associated service contract with a practical, forward-looking system and corresponding services.

The scope of supply includes the full migration of existing data (utilities: water, wastewater, electricity, natural gas, district heating). The provider will also ensure the operation (maintenance, support, etc.) of the GIS infrastructure, including the web GIS, for gwp over a basic term of 5 years, with the option to extend.
