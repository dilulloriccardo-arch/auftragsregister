Renewal and extension of Flexera’s Snow License Manager licences for 2027–2029

Public buyer
Universität Bern, Informatikdienste
17 Nov 2026, 12:00Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

The University of Bern’s IT Services have been using Flexera’s Snow License Manager as a software asset management tool (SAM tool for short) since 2021. The University of Bern now wishes to renew the licences for a further three years and is inviting relevant business partners to submit tenders. The licence period runs from 1 January 2027 to 31 December 2029.

The scope of the procurement comprises the products listed in the attached Annex 1 – Price List, namely ‘Snow Spend Optimizer – SAM’, ‘Snow Spend Optimizer – SaaS’ and ‘Data Intelligence Service’, as well as the optional ‘Flexera One IT Visibility with Technology Intelligence Platform’.

A decision on the use and final ordering of the optional product will not be made until December 2026.
