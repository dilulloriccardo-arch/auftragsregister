Partially mobile airfield lighting system including PAPI and trailer

Public buyer
armasuisse Ressourcen und Support CC WTO
11 Jan 2027, 16:00Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

Airfield lighting systems (FFBEF) are sources of light for visual identification of runways, taxiways, parking spaces and landing aids of an airfield. In order to ensure a consistently high level of availability of airfields, partially mobile lighting systems will be used if the regular airfield lighting systems fail or partially fail. The operational readiness of the Air Force of the Swiss Armed Forces will be improved with the procurement of partially mobile airfield lighting systems. On behalf of the Air Force of the Swiss Armed Forces, armasuisse will procure partially mobile airfield lighting systems including Precision Approach Path Indicator (PAPI) as well as a trailer with a total weight of max. 3.5t. The Bidder will act as General Contractor, integrate all components in the trailer and deliver a complete, operational overall system.
