Description

The overall goal of this mandate is to support the 7 thematic networks of the Swiss Agency for Development and Cooperation (SDC) in managing and publishing the content for their websites on Livingdocs and administrating the members’ collaboration platforms on Microsoft Teams, each with its own thematic focus (Agriculture & Food Systems (AFS) / Health / Water (RésEAU) / Climate, Disaster Risk Reduction & Environment (CDE) / Migration / Employment & Education (E+E) / Peace, Governance & Equality (PGE). This mandate shall start on 01.01.2025, and lasts for a period of 4 years until 31.12.2028, with an extension option of 1 additional year (01.01.2029 until 31.12.2029).
