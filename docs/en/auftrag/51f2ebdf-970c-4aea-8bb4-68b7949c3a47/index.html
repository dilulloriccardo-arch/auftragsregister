Description

Lot 1

Subject and scope of the contract (similar for all three lots, with respective thematc focus):
Description of Lot 1 (as example; Focus of Lot 2 on CDE, Focus of Lot 3 on Water)
· Thematic expertise on a wide range of thematic fields, notably agroecology, agroforestry, food systems policy and design, post-harvest, nutrition, humanitarian aid and food assistance, market systems development, international agricultural markets, private sector participation, and blended finance, rural advisory services and youth engagement;
· Knowledge and understanding of the Rome-Based Agencies (FAO, IFAD, WFP), and other international bodies, coalitions and mechanisms such as CGIAR, SUN, Agroecology Coalition,
· Knowledge / understanding of major international frameworks, tools and agreements relating to food systems and agriculture;
· Knowledge and understanding of current affairs, trends and developments related to food systems, agriculture and nutrition, as well as other topics related to food systems, in particularly climate change, risk management, health, water as well as respective thematic nexus (eg. Water-Energy-Food-Environment, WEFE);
· A professional network in relation to food systems and related topics both within Switzerland as well as abroad and linked to multilateral actors such as e.g. UN agencies, notably the RBAs. Familiarity and access to regional, national and local networks in regions where SDC is active is an asset.

Lot 2

· Thematic expertise on a wide range of thematic fields, including climate change mitigation (e.g. clean energy, low-carbon and resilient development, carbon sinks, etc.), climate change adaptation and integrated disaster risk management/reduction, environmental issues (environmental pollution, preservation and restoration of biodiverse ecosystems, natural resources management incl. forests) incl. underlying concepts, principles, standards, state of the art approaches, major technical tools and relevant literature
· Knowledge and understanding of international frameworks and processes in CDE and related topics such as sustainable development, water, food systems, desertification and others (e.g. Agenda 2030, Paris Agreement, Sendai Framework, CBD, etc.);
· Knowledge and understanding of current issues and challenges related to CDE and closely related topics such as in particular food security, health, water as well as respective thematic nexus (eg. Water-Energy-Food-Environment, WEFE)

Lot 3

· Thematic expertise embracing the water topic in many of its facets such as the following topical areas: water, sanitation and hygiene (WASH), transboundary and integrated water resources management, water governance and water diplomacy, (social) water entrepreneurship, water efficiency and effectiveness, rural and urban water supply, waste water treatment and reuse, groundwater and water pollution;
· Knowledge of the respective underlying/guiding policies, strategies, concepts, principles, standards, state of the art approaches, trends, major technical tools and relevant (recent) literature related to the above-mentioned topical areas;
· Familiarity with international frameworks, processes, bodies and key actors in the water sector such as the Agenda 2030 and the SDG 6, the international Decade for Water 2018-2028 and the related Dushanbe water process, UN Water, the World Water Forum (WWF), the annual Stockholm World Water Week (SWWW), the International Secretariat for Water (ISW), the Sanitation and Hygiene Fund (SHF), and the FDFA Guidelines on Water 2022-25
