Ultra-high Resolution Electron-beam Lithography System

Public buyer
ETH Zürich - Abt. Procurement & Export Services
10 Nov 2026, 23:58Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

The B & R Nanotechnology Center (BRNC) at ETH Zurich invites tenders for the purchase of one (1) ultra-high resolution electron-beam lithography (EBL) system. The system will be used for a variety of research activities of internal and external users, ranging from the generation of high-precision structures at the nanometer level to the rapid exposure of complex structures over large areas. The scope includes delivery, installation, training and acceptance of the system.
