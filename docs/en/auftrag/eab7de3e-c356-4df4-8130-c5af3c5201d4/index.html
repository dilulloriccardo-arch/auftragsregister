Beschaffung Druckerflotte

Public buyer
Gemeinde Möhlin
15 Jan 2027, 23:59Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

The municipality of Möhlin (AG) is procuring, by open procedure, a new printer fleet for its municipal administration and schools: 46 multifunction devices from a single manufacturer (18 A3, 28 A4) at 24 locations, on a rental basis with full service for 60 months. Included are delivery, installation, integration into the ICT environment, fleet management, maintenance, consumables, support and take-back. Print volume approx. 2.2 million pages per year. Rollout in summer 2027.
