14 Dec 2026, 16:00Submission deadline

Description

The City of Schlieren is planning a new building on the existing Zelgli school campus to expand its educational facilities and provide additional day-care facilities. The entire project will be implemented in accordance with the Minergie-A-Eco and SNBS Gold standards.

The procurement includes all planning and construction services required to carry out the necessary works in accordance with the client's requirements and objectives.
