Description

BERNMOBIL operates tram and trolleybus services with its own rail infrastructure in the City of Bern and regularly carries out construction, refurbishment and maintenance works on its overhead contact line systems.

The purpose of this tender is to procure services for the surface treatment of overhead line masts as well as welding inspections and material testing.
