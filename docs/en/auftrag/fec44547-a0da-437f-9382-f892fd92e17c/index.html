Description

The tendered services comprise the delivery and installation of a minimum of two and a maximum of four air-insulated 16 kV medium-voltage switchgear units, executed in accordance with the specifications set out in the technical requirements (Document 2), for substations in the Canton of Zurich.
The contracting authority reserves the right to waive the delivery of one or two medium-voltage switchgear units from Lot 1 (cf. Section 2 of the draft contract, Document 5).
The tendered services are defined in detail in the technical requirements (Document 2), the price schedules (Lot 1: Document 6a, Lot 2: Document 6b), and the draft contract (Document 5).
Division into lots:
Lot 1: Switchgear with a short-circuit current rating of 25 kA
(3 units, of which 2 are optional)
Lot 2: Switchgear with a short-circuit current rating of 31.5 kA
(1 unit)
