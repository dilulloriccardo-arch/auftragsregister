PES Railway Signalling Systems

Public buyer
Sihltal Zürich Uetliberg Bahn SZU AG
11 Feb 2027, 23:30Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

Sihltal Zürich Uetliberg Bahn SZU AG intends to renew its existing railway signalling systems as part of the ERTMS SZU Program (PES) and to establish the necessary conditions for the introduction of cab signalling based on ETCS Level 2.

Current Situation / Installed Base
SZU currently operates relay-based interlocking systems of the types Do55, DoC, Do67 and Do69 across its railway network. As part of PES, these existing installations are to be replaced by a modern electronic signalling system incorporating ETCS Level 2 and a Radio Block Centre (RBC).

The current signalling system concept (SA Concept) forms the planning basis for the estimated quantities of the future system. At the present planning stage, the indicative scope includes, but is not limited to, the following:
· 236 EHS;
· 32 level crossings;
· 74 points (switches);
· 291 ETCS balises;
· 291 axle counters / track circuits, before accounting for partially required redundancy;
· 233 track vacancy detection sections;
· 59 ERS;
· 11 shunting boards;
· 4 CAB boards.

These quantities reflect the current planning status and serve as the basis for the preparation of tenders. They may be adjusted during further project development and system optimisation. The detailed quantities and underlying assumptions are described in the Technical Specification and the SA Concept.

Scope of Tender / Base Contract
The scope of this tender comprises, as the base contract, the engineering, supply, integration, testing, commissioning, maintenance and documentation of the electronic interlocking systems and the Radio Block Centre (RBC) required for the introduction of ETCS Level 2.

The core systems covered by this tender shall be implemented as a fully integrated solution and incorporated into SZU's existing and future system environment.

Within the defined system boundaries, the supplier shall assume responsibility for the technical and functional integration of the systems. This includes, in particular, the integration of the electronic interlocking systems and the RBC, as well as the coordination and assurance of the defined interfaces with adjacent systems and services.

In addition to the supply of the systems, the base contract includes, in particular, all necessary engineering and design services, system integration, configuration, verification and testing, support for the preparation of safety cases and regulatory approvals, migration and commissioning, as well as the documentation and training required for operation and maintenance.

Optional Services
In addition to the base contract, the following services and systems, in particular, are included in the tender as optional items:
· ETCS balises and associated components;
· Axle counters and/or track vacancy detection systems;
· Level crossing protection systems.

Tenderers shall submit complete offers for the optional services in accordance with the tender documents. However, SZU shall be under no obligation to procure these optional services and may exercise the options individually or in combination.

For the preparation of tenders, the quantities and system boundaries defined in the technical tender documents and the pricing schedule shall apply. SZU reserves the right to adjust the final quantities during further project development.
