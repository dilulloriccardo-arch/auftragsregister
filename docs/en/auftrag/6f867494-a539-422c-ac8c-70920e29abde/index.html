Description

Lot 1

Hire of event lighting equipment and its accessories for the five areas of the Werft, Seebühne, Nord, Aussenraum and Zentral

Lot 2

Hire of event lighting equipment and its accessories for the areas of the Süd, Rote Fabrik, Zentral and a basic stock of material for last-minute changes and as a supplement for the venues.

Lot 3

Hire of event audio equipment and its accessories for the five areas of the Werft, Seebühne, Nord, and the accessories: transmitter, base and microphones.

Lot 4

Hire of event audio equipment and its accessories for the five areas of the Süd, Zentral, Zentral oben, Stammtisch, microphones and additional material.

Lot 5

Hire of event technology, video material and accessories for all venues at the festival.
