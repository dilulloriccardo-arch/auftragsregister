Operational and structural maintenance of wastewater systems

Public buyer
Gemeinde Oberkirch
5 Jan 2027, 16:00Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

For the municipality of Oberkirch, the operational and structural maintenance of the wastewater facilities must be ensured for the years 2027 to 2037, a task that entails planning, organizing, and overseeing these activities.

Each year, the condition of the facilities in a specific subarea must be assessed, and the maintenance work to be carried out in the following year must be defined. This maintenance work must be put out to tender, supervised, and documented.

The foundational data must be obtained from the GEP engineer’s database and returned to them in a suitable format for the purpose of updating.
