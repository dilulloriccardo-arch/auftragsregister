EV Enge, civil engineering contractor for utility conduits and building service connections.

Public buyer
Elektrizitätswerk der Stadt Zürich (ewz)
10 Nov 2026, 12:00Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

ewz Energy Solutions (ewz EL) has initiated TAZ projects 24208 and 24209. Within the scope of this tender, civil engineering contractor services, including flushing drilling and pipe jacking works, are being procured for the construction of the main interconnection pipeline between the lake water pumping station, the Bederstrasse energy center, and the Hürlimann site.

The tender also includes the utility trenches for the building service connections along this main interconnection pipeline.

Additional utility owners have registered their requirements as part of the electronic consultation process. These works are to be implemented in coordination with the project:
- ewz Grid
- Water Supply Zurich (WVZ)
- Energie360°

The tender is divided into two lots, which may be submitted for individually or jointly.

The costs of all participating utility owners are itemized separately in the bill of quantities and will be allocated in accordance with an agreed cost-sharing arrangement and payment
