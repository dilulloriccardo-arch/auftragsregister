Description

The object and aim of the invitation to tender is the procurement of processed InSAR data for the whole of Switzerland. The FOEN focuses on recording landslide areas and on zones containing other mass movement hazards. As the basis for monitoring surface movements throughout Switzerland, the data provided by the Sentinel-1 SAR constellation has to be processed. The processed InSAR point data may be calculated from point targets/permanent scatterers or from distributed scatterers. The detailed scope of goods/services and the requirements for the InSAR data are described in the technical specifications forming part of the tender documents.
