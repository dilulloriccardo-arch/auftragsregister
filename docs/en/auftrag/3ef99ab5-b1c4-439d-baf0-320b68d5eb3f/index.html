Description

Sihltal Zürich Uetliberg Bahn SZU AG is set to prepare and implement various infrastructure projects in the coming years (see “B2_Indicative Overview of Possible SZU Infrastructure Projects Through 2032”). To support its internal project organization, SZU is seeking experienced and capable companies to provide client support services in various technical and management roles for the years 2026–2031.

The subject of this call for bids is the conclusion of framework agreements for various lots of client representation (see C1 Framework Agreement). Specific assignments will be project-specific, based on the SZU’s needs.

Services are being solicited for the following roles or lots:
• Client representation: Overall project management (Lot 1)
• Client representation: Technical project management for track (Lot 2)
• Client representation: Technical project management for overhead contact lines (Lot 3)
• Client representation: Specialized project management for safety systems (Lot 4)
• Client representation: Specialized project management for civil engineering (Lot 5)
• Client representation: Specialized project management for electrical systems (Lot 6)
• Client representation: Specialized project management for data networks (Lot 7)
• Client representation for overall construction management (Lot 8)
• Client representation for on-site construction management (Lot 9)
• (Construction) technical support services for bid submissions by the requesting department (Lot 10)

The external client representatives support SZU in fulfilling its role as client. They represent SZU’s interests vis-à-vis designers, contractors, government agencies, third parties, and other project participants, and help ensure that projects are prepared, planned, coordinated, and implemented in accordance with the defined quality, schedule, cost, and safety objectives. For the specific scope of services to be provided for each lot, please refer to the respective specifications (LH) (see “B1_Specifications”).

The estimated annual scope of services per lot ranges from approximately 1,900 to 5,000 hours per year, depending on the respective role and expected demand. These figures are based on SZU’s current resource planning and serve solely as a non-binding estimate of anticipated needs. There is no entitlement to the provision of a specific scope of services or to an equal distribution of services among the framework agreement partners. Actual assignments will be determined by SZU’s actual project and resource needs.

The purpose of this call for proposals is to identify suitable companies that have proven experience in client representation, project management, specialized project management, senior site supervision, or on-site supervision of infrastructure and rail infrastructure projects, and that can provide flexible, reliable, and technically competent support to SZU over several years.
