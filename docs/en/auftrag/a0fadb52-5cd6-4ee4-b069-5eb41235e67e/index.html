IWB, Migration from AC450 to AC800M, Nodes 22, 32, 41, 42, and 51

Public buyer
IWB Industrielle Werke Basel
13 Nov 2026, 09:00Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

ABB AC450 process controllers are in use at the Basel Waste-to-Energy Plant (KVA). These are no longer supported or maintained by ABB; therefore, they have reached the end of their service life and must be replaced. The controller replacement will be awarded in two separate lots:

- Lot 1: Necessary hardware for controllers and I/O stations, including interfaces.

- Lot 2: Software migration from existing AC450 controllers to AC800M controllers.

The migration of the old software must be carried out without modifying the process functions.
