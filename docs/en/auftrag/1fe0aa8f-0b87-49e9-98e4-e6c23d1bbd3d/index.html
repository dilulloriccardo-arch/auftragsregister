6 piece regulating transformers 40MVA ONAN (50MVA ONAF) 110//10/20kV

Public buyer
Stadt St.Gallen, Stadtwerke
15 Jan 2027, 16:00Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

Delivery, installation, and commissioning of six regulating transformers: OS 110kV, US switchable from 10kV to 20kV, with an apparent power of 40MVA (ONAN) and 50MVA (ONAF). The OS and US windings are rated for a continuous load of 50MVA. Operation without active cooling is permitted up to 40MVA; above 40MVA, the regulating transformer must automatically engage forced cooling.

Intended Use
Regulating transformer with compensating winding

Operating Mode
Continuous Operation

Design
Power transformer with tap changer on the primary side, switchable from 10kV to 20kV undervoltage

Construction
Oil-filled transformer with air passages on the OS and US sides for outdoor or indoor installations, and road-transportable for delivery

Expected Service Life
60 years
