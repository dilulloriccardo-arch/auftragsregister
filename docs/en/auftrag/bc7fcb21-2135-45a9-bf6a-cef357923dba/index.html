Description

Overhaul of the pneumatic valves on SZU AG vehicles Be 552 511–516 (S10, Uetlibergbahn). The scope of work includes picking up the valves at the Zurich Giesshübel workshop, performing a professional overhaul (disassembly, cleaning, replacement of wear parts, reassembly, functional testing) at the contractor’s facility, and returning the overhauled valves. A total of 6 batches, each containing 180 valves, are to be overhauled.
