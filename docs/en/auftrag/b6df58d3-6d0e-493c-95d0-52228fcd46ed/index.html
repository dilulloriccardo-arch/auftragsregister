789 New Building Schoolhouse & Triple Kindergarten Malerva Sargans - Sub-object 2 (TO2) Schoolhouse - CFC 224 Roofing Works

Public buyer

27 Nov 2026, 23:59Submission deadline

Description

The Malerva new construction project comprises a schoolhouse with a gymnasium, a triple kindergarten, and an outdoor sports field including the surrounding grounds.

In addition, the day-lighting (opening up) of the Töbelibach stream will be implemented in parallel.

TO 1 – Kindergarten
TO 2 – Schoolhouse
TO 3 – Sports field
TO 4 – Töbelibach
This tender only considers sub-object TO 2 - Schoolhouse.
