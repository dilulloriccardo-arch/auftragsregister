Brief description of the building New construction of the Riet safety and rescue base in Wangs. The three-story building with a pitched roof is primarily constructed using timber framing. The foundation slab, strip footings, columns, and stairwell cores are made of cast-in-place concrete. Brief description of the work to be carried out: Roof cladding for pitched roofs using thin sheet metal and all other plumbing and tinsmith works. Fall protection systems, SHEV (Smoke and Heat Exhaust Ventilation) openings, external lightning protection.
