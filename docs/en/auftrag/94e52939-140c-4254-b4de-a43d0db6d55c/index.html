Description

With the «Vielfältige Zürcher Gewässer» programme (hereinafter VZG), the Canton of Zurich promotes biodiversity in streams and ponds and enhances their ecosystems. Through nature-oriented design and management, valuable habitats for animals and plants are to be created. To process applications submitted under the VZG programme, the AWEL Water Engineering Division relies on qualified external support provided by the so-called Coordinators for Aquatic Environments (hereinafter GB).

The GB act as the operational link between applicants, the relevant cantonal specialist units and the VZG programme management within AWEL (hereinafter PL). They carry out all preparatory work as part of the application process, enabling the PL to conduct the final assessment with minimal effort. They support the projects throughout their duration, up to and including project completion and the submission of the payment request.

Details of the services to be provided by the GB are set out in the Specifications for the Coordinators for Aquatic Environments, which form part of the tender documents, as well as in the framework agreement to be concluded with the successful tenderers.
