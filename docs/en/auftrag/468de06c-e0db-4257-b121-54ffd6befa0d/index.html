Description

The University of Applied Sciences Western Switzerland (HES-SO) comprises 28 schools located in the seven cantons of Western Switzerland. A tertiary institution with a vocational focus and more than 21,000 students, HES-SO is the largest university of applied sciences in Switzerland. A member of swissuniversities, it is a recognized player in the Swiss and international higher education landscape and plays an important role in socio-economic and cultural development. As part of a major programme to transform its information system, HES-SO is looking to acquire a solution for managing student admissions. The number of applications processed per academic year is between 10,000 and 12,000 for all Bachelor's and Master's degree programmes.
