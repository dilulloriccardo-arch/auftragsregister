SWW Moos Überbrückungsmassnahmen, PW Horn, Elektroinstallationen

Public buyer
Stadt Zürich Wasserversorgung
11 Jan 2027, 10:00Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

The Horn raw water pumping station is currently being operated via a temporary electrical infrastructure. Following the remediation of contaminants and the implementation of the necessary structural measures, all electrical installations will be completely renewed and rebuilt in accordance with current standards and fire safety requirements.

Throughout the construction period, the pumping station will remain in operation via the existing temporary system, which means that the raw water supply must be guaranteed at all times. This places high demands on the planning, coordination and execution of the works.

The plant shutdown scheduled for autumn 2027 will be used to switch the temporary operation over to the newly installed systems and control systems. During this period, the new infrastructure will be commissioned, the necessary switchover procedures carried out and the final functional tests conducted to lay the foundations for future routine operation.
