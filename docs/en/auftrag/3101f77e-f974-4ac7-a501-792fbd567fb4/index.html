4 Dec 2026, 12:00Submission deadline

Description

The tender comprises the supply and installation of ceramic wall tiles for the new «Wohnen am Stadtpark» senior citizens’ centre in Schlieren, which includes 60 senior apartments, 24 care rooms, a restaurant and ancillary rooms.

The basement, ground floor and first floor will be constructed using conventional solid construction methods, while floors 2 to 5 will be built using timber construction.

The detailed scope of work is set out in the tender documents.
