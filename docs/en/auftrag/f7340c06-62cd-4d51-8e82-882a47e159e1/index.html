Wald wastewater treatment plant, removal of micropollutants: ozonation equipment

Public buyer
Gemeinde Wald ZH, Abteilung Infrastruktur
29 Jan 2027, 12:00Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

Supply, installation and commissioning of an ozonation plant (ozone production 0.1 to 2.0 kg O3/h from oxygen, container, cooling circuit, ozone gas piping and ozone injection, residual ozone destructor, measurement and control with Profinet interface) for the Wald wastewater treatment plant, canton of Zurich. Open procedure. Deadline for questions: 15 January 2027 (simap forum). Deadline for tenders: 29 January 2027, 12:00, submitted via simap only. Language of the procedure and tenders: German.
