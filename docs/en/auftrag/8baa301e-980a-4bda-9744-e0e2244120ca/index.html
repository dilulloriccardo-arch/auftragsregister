Framework Agreement for Track Crew Reinforcement – Overhead Contact Lines & Vehicles

Public buyer
Sihltal Zürich Uetliberg Bahn SZU AG
21 Dec 2026, 16:00Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

ENG: The subject of this tender is the provision of resources (additional track crew personnel) to support SZU in carrying out work on overhead contact line systems as well as return current and earthing lines (S+E).

Upon request, the Contractor shall provide SZU with the required personnel resources (staff leasing) as well as the necessary vehicles, machinery and materials (vehicle and equipment rental).

The following overview outlines the main service areas covered by the framework agreement. Individual services will be procured through call-offs during the contract term.

The services include, in particular:
- Overhead contact line systems
- Return current and earthing lines (S+E)
- Vehicles and machinery
- Materials

The personnel deployed and the resources provided must comply with the requirements set out in the specifications. All services shall be performed in accordance with all applicable legal, technical, operational, safety and quality regulations. SZU's operating regulations are binding for the performance of the services and shall be given equal consideration.

Detailed information on the scope of services can be found in document B1_Lastenheft_techn_Spezifikation_RV Rottenverstärkung. The specifications form the basis of the framework agreement.

Subject to the bidders meeting the required quality criteria, the objective is to award framework agreements to an expected total of two framework agreement partners.
