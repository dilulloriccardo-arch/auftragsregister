IMPACT / HIMB – Supply of steel plates and machined steel components

Public buyer
Paul Scherrer Institut (PSI)
20 Jan 2027, 23:59Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

The Paul Scherrer Institute PSI invites tenders for the manufacture and supply of steel plates and processed steel components for the IMPACT / HIMB steel shielding.

The scope includes material supply, cutting and processing according to drawings, slag removal, sandblasting, marking, inspection and material certificates, packaging and transport. Assembly and welding are excluded.

Delivery stages 1–4, with an estimated total weight of 119,500 kg, will be ordered jointly and delivered in stages. Two additional options are included: Option 1 with approximately 205,500 kg and Option 2 with approximately 193,000 kg. Binding unit prices and transport prices must be quoted for both options. The options may be ordered until the end of 2028, without any obligation to order.

Tender documents are available on SIMAP in German. Bids must be submitted in German through SIMAP by 20 January 2027, 12:00 noon (CET).
