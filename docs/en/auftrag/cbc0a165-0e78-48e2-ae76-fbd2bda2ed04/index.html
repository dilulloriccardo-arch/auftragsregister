Grimsel 4 Pumped-Storage Power Station, Lot 1 for the reinforcement of the headwater channel and Lot 2 for the underwater gates

Public buyer
Kraftwerke Oberhasli AG
22 Dec 2026, 16:00Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

KWO is inviting tenders in two lots for the supply of armour for the headwater channel and two sets of double underwater gates for the Grimsel 4 pumped-storage power station.

The tender covers the design, manufacture, supply, installation and commissioning of the drive waterway armouring on individual sections of the drive waterway, as well as a total of four underwater gates (2x2) on the submerged side of the suction pipes for the two pump-turbines.
