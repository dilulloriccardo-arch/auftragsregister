Description

One 600 MHz ssNMR spectrometer Associated highly sensitive CP-MAS ssNMR probes with cryogenically cooled electronics dedicated for NMR experiments with tunable frequency (desired range will be disclosed on request) on 1st channel and tunable to 1H NMR frequency on the 2nd channel. The supplier must provide proof that a device largely corresponding to the NMR system described above has already been installed at a customer's premises (for consultation purposes, contact details of the customer or literature references of publicly published data must be provided). No partial offers of the goods above listed will be accepted.
