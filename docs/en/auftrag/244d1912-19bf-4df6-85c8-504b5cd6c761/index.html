Rüeggisingerstrasse, Gersagplatz – Hammer roundabout, renovation and redesign

Public buyer
Gemeinde Emmen - Departement Tiefbau und Werke
11 Jan 2027, 16:00Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

Rüeggisingerstrasse is a first-class municipal road that plays a central role for pedestrians, cyclists, public bus services, and private motorized traffic within both the local and regional networks. The section of the road between Gersagplatz and the Hammer roundabout (Section 02) is in need of renovation and no longer meets the requirements for modern streetscape design. Furthermore, buses frequently get caught in traffic congestion during the evening peak hours, which leads to instability across the entire bus network and causes delays. This project aims to achieve the following objectives:

• Preservation of existing road infrastructure
• Renewal and optimization of street lighting (conversion to LED)
• Modern design of the streetscape and enhancement of the quality of the public realm
• Improvement of traffic safety for all road users through measures such as a 30 km/h speed limit, pedestrian refuge islands, raised sidewalk crossings, wider sidewalks, and improved visibility
• Optimization of intersections by adjusting right-of-way rules, ensuring traffic capacity, and addressing the accident hotspot at the "Hammer" roundabout
• Prioritization and promotion of public transport through bus lanes, in-lane bus stops, and the upgrading of bus stops to ensure compliance with the Disability Equality Act (BehiG)
• Provision of continuous and intuitive routing for cyclists, supported by appropriate infrastructure
• Reduction of noise emissions for the local population, ensuring compliance with noise exposure limits at all assessment points within the project area
• Reduction of "rat-running" (through-traffic) in residential neighborhoods
• Ecological enhancement of the streetscape through the planting of new trees, the removal of impermeable surfaces (desealing) in parking areas and residual spaces, and the integration of "sponge city" elements
