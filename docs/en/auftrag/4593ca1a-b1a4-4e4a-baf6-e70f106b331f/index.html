Supply of API standard pipes and pup joints for Bäumlihof stage 6

Public buyer
Schweizer Salinen AG
5 Jan 2027, 23:45Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

Contracting authority: Schweizer Salinen AG, Pratteln, Switzerland
Subject: Supply of API 5CT steel pipes (tubing and casing, grade J55) and pup joints for the Bäumlihof brine field, stage 6, Möhlin (AG). Basic order: 4½" casing, 1,000 m; 5½" casing, 2,500 m (two alternative premium gas-tight connection types, not cumulative); pup joints. Options: 3½" tubing, 2,500 m; additional 4½" casing, 1,500 m; 6⅝" casing, 600 m, each with the corresponding pup joints. Delivery DAP Riburg plant, Riburgerstrasse 1507, CH-4313 Möhlin (Incoterms 2020).

Deadline for tenders: 5 January 2027, 23:45 (local time)
Tender documents: available free of charge on www.simap.ch. Questions via the simap.ch forum until 27 November 2026.

Language of the procedure: German
