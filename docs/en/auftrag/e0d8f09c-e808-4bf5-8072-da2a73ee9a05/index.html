Procurement of a New ERP Solution

Public buyer
Zentrum für Labormedizin
18 Dec 2026, 23:59Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

The Centre for Laboratory Medicine (Zentrum für Labormedizin, ZLM), an independent public-law institution of the Canton of St. Gallen, is procuring an integrated ERP solution close to standard to replace its existing SAP solution and the ancillary system ProCommerce (purchasing and warehouse).

Subject of the procurement
- Licences or rights of use for the standard software
- Implementation of the solution at a fixed price (design, configuration, interfaces, data migration, testing, training, go-live on 1 January 2028)
- Maintenance, further development and support for the duration of the contract

Functional scope
- Order-to-cash: transfer of laboratory orders and services from the laboratory information systems, tariff application (including the Swiss Federal Office of Public Health's List of Analyses), invoicing, accounts receivable management and dunning
- Accounts payable and purchasing (source-to-pay)
- Warehouse and logistics, including batch and expiry date management
- Financial accounting, fixed asset accounting and cost accounting, controlling and reporting in accordance with Swiss GAAP FER
- Master data, interfaces (via the Mirth middleware) and migration
- On-premises operation in the HOCH IT data centre, with the option of a later move to the cloud

Options (not evaluated): "Projects and project costs" and "Integrated web shop".

Not included: HR and payroll, CRM, laboratory information systems, sample logistics and waste disposal.
