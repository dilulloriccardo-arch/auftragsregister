Procurement of an Automation Platform for Enzyme Engineering (APEE)

Public buyer
Universität Bern
15 Jan 2027, 23:59Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

The automation platform for enzyme engineering must be provided with all hardware, software, and system controls required for high-throughput screening of enzyme libraries. The offering must include a fully integrated, turnkey solution. Furthermore, the platform must enable an automated workflow capable of identifying enzyme variants that efficiently catalyze selected chemical transformations. The system must be capable of performing the steps described in the documentation fully automatically.

As part of this workflow, it must be possible to process at least 24 microtiter plates (96-well) per week (see the “Workflow” document). The products listed in the request for proposals are examples intended to illustrate the required performance level. Equivalent products are permitted; the supplier must demonstrate equivalence.

Since the APEE will be operated by both experienced and less experienced users, high operational efficiency, high operational stability, and high user-friendliness of both hardware and software are of central importance. In addition, the system’s dimensions must fit within the laboratory space available for this purpose. The available room dimensions are specified in the eligibility criteria.
