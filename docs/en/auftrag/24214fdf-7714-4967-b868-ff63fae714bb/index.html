Description

University Hospital Basel (USB) is renewing its Oracle Exadata infrastructure across two data centers due to the upcoming end of support.

The procurement includes the delivery, installation, configuration, and commissioning of the new infrastructure by the end of March 2027, as well as Oracle hardware and software support for five years. Data migration will be carried out by USB.
