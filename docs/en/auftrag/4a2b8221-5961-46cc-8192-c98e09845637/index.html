Description

Lot 1

Lots 1 to 5 will be awarded in the form of framework agreements.

For lot 1, a maximum of five tenderers will be awarded framework agreements, each for the total of 1’200 days (approx. 240 days per year).

For Lot 1, the contracting authority reserves the right to award multiple contracts. The number of tenders awarded per lot is listed in Chapter 2.4 in the tender document.

The contracts are concluded subject to the prior approval of credits by the FDFA.

Lot 2

Lots 1 to 5 will be awarded in the form of framework agreements.

For lots 2-5, a maximum of one tenderer will be awarded a framework agreement for the total number of days / days per year of the specific lot.

For lot 2: Scope 750 days in total for the period of 2025 to 2029

The contract is concluded subject to the prior approval of credits by the FDFA.

Lot 3

Lots 1 to 5 will be awarded in the form of framework agreements.

Lot 4

Lots 1 to 5 will be awarded in the form of framework agreements.

For lots 2-5, a maximum of one tenderer will be awarded a framework agreement for the total number of days / days per year of the specific lot.

For lot 4: Scope 800 days in total for the period of 2025 to 2029
