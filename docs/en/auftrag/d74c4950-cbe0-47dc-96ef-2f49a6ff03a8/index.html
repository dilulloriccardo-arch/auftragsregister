New School Building with Underground Parking / Integration of Kindergarten Units – General Planning Team Selection Procedure

The submission deadline has passed (30 Oct 2026). If the contract is awarded and published on simap.ch, you’ll see here who won it.

30 Oct 2026Submission deadline
Documents and official publication on simap.chAdd the deadline to your calendarReport an error

Description

As part of its school facilities planning, the municipality examined various options for the future development of the Leuggern school site. After carefully considering the different options, the Municipal Council decided to centralise the school facilities through the construction of a new school building at the current secondary school site, including an underground car park, together with the renovation of the primary school building in Leuggern.

The purpose of this procedure is to select a general planning team (GP team) that can be commissioned by the municipality to undertake the planning and implementation of the project.
