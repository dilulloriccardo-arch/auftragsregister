Glattalbahn Track Infrastructure – Procurement of Twin-Block Sleepers for OBE GTB1 and GTB2A

The submission deadline has passed (30 Oct 2026, 12:00). If the contract is awarded and published on simap.ch, you’ll see here who won it.

Description

The subject matter of this procurement is the manufacture and supply of twin-block sleepers, including fastening systems and chamber filler elements, in accordance with the tender documents for the construction of a slab track in a grassed track section.

The contracting authority is awarding a contract for the manufacture and supply of track components for the period from 2027 to 2033.

The contract is divided into the following stages:
- Stage 1: OBE GTB1, Leutschenbach - Glattpark (2027)
- Stage 2: OBE GTB1, Glattpark - Zürich Flughafen (2028 - 2029)
- Stage 3: OBE GTB1, Auzelg - Stettbach (2029 - 2032)
- Stage 4: GTB2A, Verlängerung Glattalbahn (2029 - 2033)

All stages will be evaluated and awarded together. The exact scope of services is set out in the tender documents.
