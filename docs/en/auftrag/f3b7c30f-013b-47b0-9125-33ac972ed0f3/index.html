Description

SUPSI IT Service intends to identify a supplier for the expansion of the HPC cluster in service, purchased through public tender IT001 in 2020 and already expanded in the following two years.

These specifications cover:

- The acquisition of four new computing nodes to be integrated into the existing infrastructure, ensuring full compatibility and management using the administration and monitoring tools already in use.

- The purchase of an installation, configuration, and integration service for the current control and management infrastructure.

- The optional signing of a support and management contract for one year, renewable.
