Description

The focus of the contracting agreement is on providing a continuous supply of useful heat using a mobile wood pellet system and the associated operational services throughout the entire contract term. The scope of services is divided into the following main areas: planning and construction, provision, energy supply, operation and maintenance (including 24/7 on-call service), and decommissioning.
